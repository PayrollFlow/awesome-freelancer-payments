## Traditional Money Transfer

- **SWIFT bank wire** — Universal but slow (1–5 business days) and often carries intermediary-bank fees.
- **SEPA (Euro area)** — Fast and cheap for EUR transfers within the SEPA zone.
- **ACH (US)** — Low-cost domestic US transfers; not designed for cross-border.

## Stablecoin & Crypto Payouts

Getting paid in **USDT** (Tether) — a stablecoin pegged 1:1 to the US dollar — settles in minutes and avoids crypto price volatility. Ideal when bank transfers where you live are slow, costly, or unreliable.

- **[PayrollFlow](https://payrollflow.io)** — Withdraw your balance to USDT on TRC20 / ERC20 / BEP20 with free withdrawals and a human review step before each payout.
- **Self-custody wallets** (Trust Wallet, MetaMask, Tron wallets) — You hold the keys and receive USDT directly on-chain. Always match the network to the address.
- **Exchange accounts** (Binance, OKX, Bybit, etc.) — Receive USDT, convert, and cash out to local currency where supported.

## Understanding USDT Networks (TRC20 / ERC20 / BEP20)

USDT exists on multiple blockchains. The three most common for payouts:

| Network | Chain | Typical fee | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------------|-------|
| **TRC20** | Tron | Lowest | Cheapest and fast; the common default for USDT payouts |
| **BEP20** | BNB Smart Chain | Low | Fast and inexpensive; widely supported by exchanges |
| **ERC20** | Ethereum | Higher / variable | Most widely supported, but gas fees rise when Ethereum is busy |

> ⚠️ **The one rule that matters most:** the network must match. USDT sent on TRC20 must go to a TRC20 address, ERC20 to an ERC20 address, BEP20 to a BEP20 address. Send to the wrong network and the funds can be **permanently lost.** Always confirm the network before sharing an address.

See the full breakdown in the **[USDT Payments Handbook](https://github.com/PayrollFlow/usdt-payments-handbook)**.

## Fees & Safety Checklist

- **Compare the effective rate**, not just the headline fee — FX spread often costs more than the stated transfer fee.
- **Copy-paste or scan wallet addresses** — never type them by hand.
- **Confirm the network** (TRC20 / ERC20 / BEP20) before every crypto payment.
- **Send a small test amount** for a new address or a large payment.
- **Keep records** — invoice, transaction hash/reference, date, and amount, for tax.
- On-chain transfers are **irreversible** — there is no chargeback, so verify the recipient.
